# Module 1: AI Project Supervisor

## Step 1: AI-Powered Project Idea Discovery

**A. Identify Student Needs**
- If a student has no project idea, AI asks about their domain, target users or geographical region, interests, skills, budget, and available resources.
- If a student already has an idea, they can enter it directly and proceed to Step 2.

**B. Market and Problem Research**
- AI researches current industry trends, user pain points, existing solutions, and unsolved problems using relevant websites, research papers, reports, and other credible sources.
- It identifies market gaps and evaluates problems from the target users’ perspective.
- Each finding should include supporting sources and references.

**C. Project Recommendations**
AI generates ranked project ideas, explaining each idea’s problem statement, target users, proposed solution, existing alternatives, innovation, feasibility, required technologies, expected outcomes, and potential risks.

Students can select a suggested idea or submit their own to continue.

## Step 2: Project Analysis and Planning

**A. Requirement Analysis**
- AI analyzes the selected idea and any requirements provided by the student.
- It identifies missing information, asks clarifying questions, defines project scope, and establishes measurable deliverables and acceptance criteria.

**B. Timeline and Methodology**
- AI asks about the deadline, daily availability, team size, skills, and constraints.
- It recommends an appropriate development methodology, such as Agile/Scrum, Kanban, or Waterfall, based on project complexity and requirements.
- It generates a realistic timeline and flags deadlines that appear infeasible.

**C. Team Formation**
- Students can work individually or invite team members.
- Invited members join after accepting the invitation.
- Students can designate a team leader to manage assignments and coordinate work.

## Step 3: AI-Generated Execution Plan

- AI divides the project into phases, sprints where appropriate, and individual tasks.
- Each task includes a description, priority, estimated effort, dependencies, assigned student, deadline, and completion criteria.
- Team leaders can assign tasks manually; otherwise, AI recommends and distributes tasks according to skills, workload, and availability.
- Every phase, sprint, and task has a deadline and a defined completion status.

## Step 4: Progress Monitoring and Delivery

- AI tracks task completion, overdue work, blockers, workload, and overall project progress.
- It alerts students and team leaders to delays and recommends corrective actions.
- Students submit deliverables through GitHub repository links and supporting evidence.
- AI verifies submitted evidence against task acceptance criteria and flags incomplete or unverifiable work.

## Step 5: Project Completion and Certification

Once all required deliverables are submitted and the completion criteria are satisfied, the system generates a verifiable project completion certificate containing the student's name, project title, completion date, and unique certificate ID.
