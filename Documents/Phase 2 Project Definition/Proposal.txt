An AI-Powered Platform for Project Idea Discovery, Planning, Progress Monitoring and
Certification
Goal: To guide students through their entire project journey, from idea discovery and planning
to progress tracking, evidence-based verification and a verifiable completion certificate, using an
AI supervisor.
Overview: AI Project Supervisor is a web-based platform that acts as a virtual supervisor for student
projects. It guides students from finding a project idea, through analysis, planning and execution, to
monitored progress, verified delivery and a completion certificate.
Objective: To build an AI supervisor that helps students discover and validate project ideas, plan them
with clear tasks and deadlines, track progress, verify submitted deliverables against acceptance criteria, and
issue a verifiable project completion certificate.
Motivation: Students often struggle to choose a viable idea, plan realistically and coordinate in teams,
while supervisors cannot monitor every project continuously. Running a small language model locally
keeps the solution affordable and private.
Scope of the Project: Students without an idea are guided by AI through their domain, interests, skills
and resources, and receive ranked, researched ideas; students with an idea enter it directly. The AI then
analyzes requirements, recommends a methodology (Agile/Scrum, Kanban or Waterfall), builds a timeline
and execution plan, supports individual or team work, tracks progress and delays, and verifies GitHub-
based deliverables before a certificate is generated.
Target Users: Students working individually or in teams, team leaders, and project supervisors.
Tools & Technologies Used: Ollama (model runner), Llama 3.2 3B (small language model), Node.js,
Express, MongoDB, Python, Scikit-learn, LangChain or LlamaIndex (RAG), GitHub.
Expected Outcomes/Result: An intelligent web-based AI supervisor that helps students find ideas, plan
and execute projects, monitors their progress, verifies submitted work and issues verifiable completion
certificates.
Future Scope: Support for additional evidence sources, supervisor analytics dashboards, multilingual
support and further modules of the platform.
Which real world problem shall be solved by this project?
Students lack guided support in choosing a project idea, planning it realistically, coordinating their team
and staying on schedule, and supervisors have no scalable way to track progress or verify that submitted
work meets requirements. This project provides an AI supervisor that guides, tracks and verifies student
projects from start to certified completion.
