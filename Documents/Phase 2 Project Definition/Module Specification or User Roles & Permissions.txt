 02. Complete Module Specification
 Module Name: AI Project Supervisor
 Module Description:
 The AI Project Supervisor is the core module of the platform. It acts as a virtual project supervisor,
 guiding students through project idea discovery, requirement analysis, project planning, team
 formation, task distribution, progress monitoring, deliverable verification, and project completion
 certification.
 The module uses a locally hosted Small Language Model (SLM) to provide intelligent
 recommendations and project supervision. It also uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to
 retrieve relevant information from credible external sources and Python-based Machine Learning
 techniques where applicable.

 2.1 Module Components
 The AI Project Supervisor consists of the following five major components:
   1. AI-Powered Project Idea Discovery
   2. Project Analysis and Planning
   3. AI-Generated Project Execution Plan
   4. Progress Monitoring and Deliverable Verification
   5. Project Completion and Certification


 2.2 Detailed Module Specification

 2.2.1 Component 1: AI-Powered Project Idea Discovery
 Purpose: To help students discover suitable project ideas based on their interests, skills, available
 resources, and real-world problems.

 A. Student Requirement Collection
    • The system shall allow students to start a new project.
    • The system shall provide two options:
        – Find a project idea using AI.
        – Continue with an existing project idea.
    • If a student does not have a project idea, AI shall initiate an interactive conversation.
    • AI shall collect the following information:
        – Preferred project domain.
        – Personal interests.
        – Technical skills and experience.
        – Target users.
        – Target geographical region.
        – Available budget.
        – Available hardware, software, and other resources.

        – Project preferences and constraints.
    • The system shall allow students to review and update their provided information.
    • If a student already has a project idea, the system shall allow direct submission without
       completing the idea discovery questionnaire.

 B. Market and Problem Research
    • AI shall analyze the student's requirements and identify relevant real-world problems.
    • The system shall research:
        – Current industry trends.
        – Existing products and solutions.
        – User problems and pain points.
        – Market demands.
        – Research gaps.
        – Opportunities for innovation.
    • The system shall retrieve relevant information from:
        – Research papers.
        – Academic publications.
        – Industry reports.
        – Relevant websites.
        – Other credible online sources.
    • RAG shall retrieve relevant information to support AI-generated responses.
    • AI shall compare existing solutions and identify limitations.
    • AI shall evaluate identified problems from the target users' perspectives.
    • Each research-based finding shall include supporting references and source links.
    • The system shall distinguish between sourced information and AI-generated suggestions.

 C. Project Idea Recommendation
    • AI shall generate multiple project ideas based on the collected requirements and research.
    • The system shall rank recommended ideas according to:
        – Relevance to student interests.
        – Technical feasibility.
        – Available resources.
        – Innovation potential.
        – Real-world usefulness.
        – Estimated complexity.
    • Each recommended idea shall contain:
        – Project title.
        – Problem statement.
        – Target users.
        – Proposed solution.
        – Existing alternatives.
        – Innovation and uniqueness.
        – Required technologies.

        – Required resources.
        – Feasibility analysis.
        – Expected outcomes.
        – Potential risks and challenges.
        – Supporting references.
    • Students shall be able to view and compare recommended ideas.
    • Students shall be able to select an AI-recommended idea.
    • Students shall also be able to submit their own project idea.
    • The selected idea shall be saved and forwarded to Project Analysis and Planning.

 Business Rules:
   1. A project idea must be provided before proceeding to project analysis.
   2. Students may use an AI-generated idea or submit their own.
   3. AI recommendations must be relevant to the student's collected requirements.
   4. Research-based claims must include supporting references whenever available.
   5. Selecting an idea shall not automatically finalize the project scope.

 2.2.2 Component 2: Project Analysis and Planning
 Purpose: To analyze the selected project idea, define its requirements, establish project boundaries,
 recommend a development methodology, and prepare a realistic project timeline.

 A. Requirement Analysis
    • AI shall analyze the selected project idea.
    • Students shall be able to provide additional project requirements.
    • AI shall identify functional requirements.
    • AI shall identify non-functional requirements.
    • AI shall identify project constraints and dependencies.
    • AI shall identify missing or unclear requirements.
    • AI shall ask relevant clarification questions.
    • AI shall define the project scope.
    • AI shall identify project objectives.
    • AI shall establish measurable project deliverables.
    • AI shall generate acceptance criteria for each major deliverable.
    • Students shall be able to review, edit, and approve the analyzed requirements.
    • Approved requirements shall be stored as the project's baseline.

 B. Timeline and Development Methodology
    • The system shall collect the following planning information:
        – Project submission deadline.
        – Daily working hours or availability.
        – Team size.
        – Individual team members' skills.
        – Technical constraints.

        – Available resources.
        – Project complexity.
    • AI shall evaluate the collected information.
    • AI shall recommend an appropriate development methodology:
        – Agile/Scrum.
        – Kanban.
        – Waterfall.
    • AI shall explain why the selected methodology is suitable.
    • AI shall generate a project timeline.
    • The timeline shall include:
        – Project phases.
        – Phase start dates.
        – Phase deadlines.
        – Estimated duration.
        – Important milestones.
        – Dependencies between phases.
    • AI shall evaluate whether the proposed timeline is achievable.
    • The system shall flag unrealistic deadlines.
    • AI shall recommend adjustments if the project deadline is infeasible.
    • Students shall be able to review and approve the proposed timeline.

 C. Team Formation and Management
    • Students shall be able to choose between individual and team-based project development.
    • Students shall be able to create a project team.
    • The project creator shall initially become the team leader.
    • Team leaders shall be able to invite other registered students.
    • The system shall send invitations to selected students.
    • Invited students shall be able to accept or reject invitations.
    • Only students who accept invitations shall become team members.
    • Team leaders shall be able to view team members.
    • Team leaders shall be able to coordinate team activities.
    • Team leaders shall be able to assign or reassign tasks.
    • The system shall maintain team membership information.
    • Students shall be able to view their assigned responsibilities.

 Business Rules:
   1. Every project must have at least one student.
   2. A project can be individual or team-based.
   3. Each team shall have one designated team leader.
   4. A student must accept an invitation before becoming a team member.
   5. Only approved project requirements shall be used to generate the final execution plan.
   6. The project timeline must consider the available deadline and resources.

   7. Changes to approved requirements may require timeline and task-plan updates.

 2.2.3 Component 3: AI-Generated Project Execution Plan
 Purpose: To convert approved project requirements into a structured execution plan containing
 phases, sprints, and individual tasks.

 A. Project Phase Generation
    • AI shall analyze the approved project requirements.
    • AI shall divide the project into logical development phases.
    • Each phase shall contain:
        – Phase title.
        – Phase description.
        – Objectives.
        – Start date.
        – End date.
        – Expected deliverables.
        – Completion criteria.
        – Current status.
    • AI shall establish dependencies between phases.
    • The system shall allow students to view the complete project structure.

 B. Sprint Management
    • For projects following Agile/Scrum, AI shall divide development phases into sprints.
    • Each sprint shall contain:
        – Sprint title.
        – Sprint objectives.
        – Sprint description.
        – Start date.
        – End date.
        – Sprint deliverables.
        – Assigned tasks.
        – Completion status.
    • The system shall allow students and team leaders to view sprint progress.
    • AI shall recommend sprint durations according to project complexity and deadlines.
    • For Kanban or Waterfall projects, the system shall use the corresponding planning structure
       rather than forcing sprint-based execution.

 C. Task Generation and Management
    • AI shall generate individual tasks according to project requirements.
    • Each task shall include:
        – Task title.
        – Task description.
        – Priority level.

        – Estimated effort.
        – Dependencies.
        – Assigned student.
        – Start date.
        – Deadline.
        – Completion criteria.
        – Current status.
    • The system shall support task priorities:
        – Low.
        – Medium.
        – High.
        – Critical.
    • The system shall support task statuses:
        – To Do.
        – In Progress.
        – Under Review.
        – Completed.
        – Blocked.
    • AI shall recommend task assignments based on:
        – Team members' technical skills.
        – Individual workload.
        – Task complexity.
        – Available working hours.
    • Team leaders shall be able to assign tasks manually.
    • Team leaders shall be able to modify AI-recommended assignments.
    • Students shall be able to view their assigned tasks.
    • Students shall be able to update task progress.
    • The system shall maintain task history and status changes.

 D. Execution Plan Management
    • Students shall be able to review the complete execution plan.
    • Team leaders shall be able to modify task assignments and deadlines.
    • AI shall recommend changes when project requirements or deadlines change.
    • The system shall maintain relationships between phases, sprints, and tasks.
    • The system shall calculate completion status based on associated tasks and deliverables.

 Business Rules:
   1. Every phase must have defined objectives and completion criteria.
   2. Every generated task must have a description, priority, estimated effort, deadline, and
       completion criteria.
   3. Every task must have an assigned student before execution.
   4. Task dependencies must be respected.

   5. Team leaders can manually override AI-generated assignments.
   6. Students can update only tasks assigned to them, subject to their team permissions.
   7. A task cannot be marked Completed without satisfying its required completion criteria and
       submission requirements.
   8. Completed work must remain recorded in the project's task history.

 2.2.4 Component 4: Progress Monitoring and Deliverable Verification
 Purpose: To monitor project execution, identify delays and blockers, evaluate submitted work, and
 verify project deliverables against defined acceptance criteria.

 A. Project Progress Monitoring
    • The system shall continuously maintain project progress based on recorded task and
       deliverable updates.
    • Students shall be able to view their individual progress.
    • Team leaders shall be able to view overall team progress.
    • Supervisors shall be able to monitor assigned projects.
    • AI shall analyze:
        – Completed tasks.
        – Pending tasks.
        – Overdue tasks.
        – Blocked tasks.
        – Upcoming deadlines.
        – Individual workload.
        – Overall project progress.
    • The system shall calculate project completion percentages.
    • The system shall identify delayed phases and sprints.
    • The system shall identify team members with excessive workloads.
    • AI shall identify potential project risks.
    • The system shall maintain project progress history.

 B. Delay Detection and Recommendations
    • AI shall detect tasks that exceed their deadlines.
    • AI shall identify tasks that may affect dependent tasks.
    • The system shall alert responsible students and team leaders.
    • AI shall recommend corrective actions.
    • Recommendations may include:
        – Reassigning tasks.
        – Adjusting task deadlines.
        – Redistributing workloads.
        – Resolving identified blockers.
        – Revising the project timeline.
    • The system shall allow students to report blockers.




                                                       Page 8
AI Project Supervisor — Software Requirements Specification



    • Team leaders shall be able to review and manage reported blockers.

 C. Deliverable Submission
    • Students shall be able to submit completed work for assigned tasks.
    • The system shall support GitHub repository links as the primary project evidence source.
    • Students shall be able to provide supporting evidence, such as:
        – GitHub repository URLs.
        – Relevant commit or pull request links.
        – Screenshots.
        – Documentation.
        – Other supporting materials, where supported.
    • The system shall associate submitted evidence with its relevant project, phase, sprint, or task.
    • The system shall maintain submission history.
    • Students shall be able to resubmit evidence when requested.

 D. AI-Based Evidence Verification
    • AI shall analyze submitted deliverables.
    • The system shall compare submitted evidence against the task's acceptance criteria.
    • The system shall evaluate whether:
        – The submitted repository or evidence is accessible.
        – Relevant implementation files are available.
        – Required functionality appears to be implemented.
        – Submitted work is consistent with the task requirements.
        – Supporting evidence is sufficient.
    • The system shall identify missing or incomplete deliverables.
    • AI shall generate a verification report.
    • Each verification report shall include:
        – Submitted evidence.
        – Evaluated acceptance criteria.
        – Verification findings.
        – Identified issues.
        – Verification status.
        – Recommendations for improvement.
    • The system shall support the following verification statuses:
        – Pending Verification.
        – Verified.
        – Partially Verified.
        – Rejected.
        – Requires Resubmission.
    • Students shall be able to review verification feedback.
    • Students shall be able to correct identified issues and submit updated evidence.
    • Supervisors shall be able to review verification results and intervene when necessary.

 Business Rules:
   1. Every deliverable must be associated with an existing project task or milestone.
   2. Evidence must be submitted before a deliverable can be verified.
   3. Verification must be based on predefined acceptance criteria.
   4. Inaccessible or insufficient evidence must not automatically be considered verified.
   5. AI verification results are recommendations and must not be treated as absolute proof of
       correctness.
   6. Supervisors may review or override verification results with a recorded reason.
   7. A task shall not qualify as verified completion until its required evidence satisfies the
       applicable criteria.
   8. Previous submissions and verification results must remain available in the project history.

 2.2.5 Component 5: Project Completion and Certification
 Purpose: To evaluate project completion and generate a unique, verifiable project completion
 certificate for eligible students.

 A. Project Completion Evaluation
    • The system shall evaluate overall project completion.
    • AI shall analyze the status of required project phases, sprints, tasks, and deliverables.
    • The system shall verify that all mandatory project requirements have been fulfilled.
    • The system shall identify pending tasks.
    • The system shall identify missing or unverified deliverables.
    • The system shall identify unresolved project blockers.
    • The system shall generate a final project completion report.
    • The system shall determine whether the project satisfies its completion criteria.

 B. Certificate Generation
    • The system shall generate a project completion certificate when all mandatory completion
       conditions are satisfied.
    • The certificate shall contain:
        – Student's full name.
        – Project title.
        – Project completion date.
        – Unique certificate ID.
        – Certificate verification information.
    • For team-based projects, each eligible team member shall receive an individual certificate.
    • The system shall store generated certificates in the database.
    • Students shall be able to view and download their certificates.

 C. Certificate Verification
    • Every certificate shall have a unique identification number.
    • The system shall provide a certificate verification facility.
    • Users shall be able to enter a certificate ID to verify its authenticity.



                                                       Page 10
AI Project Supervisor — Software Requirements Specification



    • The system shall display the certificate's validity status.
    • The system shall display relevant certificate information.
    • The system shall prevent duplicate certificate IDs.
    • Sub Admins shall be able to manage certificate records.

 Business Rules:
   1. Certificates shall only be generated for projects satisfying all mandatory completion criteria.
   2. Every required deliverable must have an accepted verification status.
   3. No mandatory task may remain incomplete.
   4. Each certificate must have a unique certificate ID.
   5. Team members must satisfy the applicable individual completion requirements.
   6. Certificate records must be permanently associated with their respective students and projects.
   7. Certificate verification must not require access to the student's private project workspace.

 03. User Roles & Permissions

 3.1 Overview of User Roles
 The AI Project Supervisor module shall support seven primary user roles. Each role shall have
 specific responsibilities and access permissions.

 Role                         Description
 Student                      Creates projects, discovers ideas, participates in project execution and submits
                              deliverables.
 Team Leader                  Manages a team, coordinates members, assigns tasks and monitors team progress.
 Team Member                  Participates in team projects, completes assigned tasks and submits evidence.
 Supervisor                   Monitors assigned projects, reviews progress and verifies project completion.
 Super Admin                  Holds the highest authority on the platform, inspects all platform data and activity,
                              manages users and creates Sub Admins by promoting or demoting users.
 Sub Admin                    Manages projects, teams, configurations and system-wide operations of the
                              platform, as delegated by the Super Admin.
 AI Supervisor                Provides automated guidance, analysis, planning, monitoring and verification.

 Important distinction: The AI Supervisor is an automated system role, not a human account. Its
 actions are performed through the AI services under the permissions and rules configured by the
 platform.

    • Provide project requirements.

 3.2.5 Super Admin
 Description:
 The Super Admin is the highest-authority role on the platform. The Super Admin has oversight
 access to inspect everything on the platform, manages all platform users, and creates Sub Admins
 by promoting users to the Sub Admin role or removes Sub Admin privileges by demoting them. All
 other platform administration work is performed by Sub Admins.
 Permissions:
 User Management
    • View registered users.
    • Search and filter users.
    • View user profiles.
    • Activate or deactivate user accounts.
    • Assign and update user roles.
    • Manage account access.
    • Monitor user activity.
 Sub Admin Management
    • Create Sub Admins by promoting an existing registered user to the Sub Admin role.
    • Remove Sub Admin privileges by demoting a Sub Admin to a regular user role.
    • View all Sub Admins and their assigned privileges.
    • Monitor and review Sub Admin activity.
    • Review the records of all promotions and demotions.
 Platform Oversight
    • Inspect all projects, teams, tasks, submitted evidence and verification reports.
    • Inspect all certificate records and certificate verification activity.
    • Inspect AI configurations, AI service status and system logs.
    • Inspect platform usage statistics and system reports.
    • Inspect all actions performed by Sub Admins.
 Restrictions:
    • Super Admin access must be authenticated and authorized.
    • All user account changes, role changes, promotions and demotions must be logged.
    • Sensitive user information must only be accessed when necessary for authorized
       administrative purposes.
    • The Super Admin cannot be promoted, demoted or deactivated by a Sub Admin.

 3.2.7 AI Supervisor
 Description:
 The AI Supervisor is the intelligent component of the platform. It acts as a virtual supervisor and
 assists students, team leaders and human supervisors throughout the project lifecycle.
 Permissions and Functional Responsibilities:
 Project Idea Discovery
    • Collect student requirements.
    • Analyze interests, skills and available resources.
    • Research industry trends and user problems.
    • Identify market gaps.
    • Generate ranked project recommendations.
    • Provide feasibility analysis.
    • Provide research references.
 Project Analysis
    • Analyze selected project ideas.
    • Identify missing requirements.
    • Ask clarification questions.
    • Generate project scope.
    • Define measurable deliverables.
    • Establish acceptance criteria.
 Project Planning
    • Recommend development methodologies.
    • Generate project timelines.
    • Identify potential scheduling problems.
    • Divide projects into phases.
    • Generate sprints where appropriate.
    • Generate individual tasks.
    • Recommend task priorities and deadlines.
    • Recommend task assignments.

 Progress Monitoring
    • Analyze task completion.
    • Detect overdue tasks.
    • Identify project delays.

 Deliverable Verification
    • Analyze submitted GitHub evidence.

    • Generate verification reports.
    • Recommend resubmission when necessary.
 Project Completion
    • Evaluate project completion criteria.
    • Generate final project reports.
    • Determine certificate eligibility.
    • Request human supervisor approval where required.
 Restrictions:
    • AI cannot independently modify Super Admin or Sub Admin permissions.
    • AI cannot access projects without authorized context.
    • AI cannot override human supervisor decisions.
    • AI cannot automatically treat inaccessible evidence as verified.
    • AI cannot issue certificates unless all configured completion conditions are satisfied.
    • AI recommendations and verification decisions must be recorded for traceability.

 3.4 General Business Rules for User Roles
   1. Every user must have an authenticated account before accessing protected platform features.
   2. Every user must have an assigned role.
   3. Users shall only access functionality permitted by their assigned role.
   4. A student who creates a team-based project shall initially become its team leader.
   5. A team member must accept an invitation before joining a team.
   6. Only one designated team leader shall manage a particular project team at a time.
   7. A student may participate in individual or team-based projects.
   8. Team leaders may assign and reassign tasks within their project teams.
   9. Team members may update the progress of their assigned tasks.
   10. Supervisors may monitor and review only authorized projects.
   11. The Super Admin shall have system-wide oversight and user management permissions, and
       shall be the only role able to create Sub Admins by promoting users or remove Sub Admin
       privileges by demoting them.
   12. Sub Admins shall have platform operation management permissions, excluding user
       management and Sub Admin management.
   13. AI operations must follow the permissions and project access restrictions of the requesting
       user.
   14. All important project changes, verification decisions, promotions, demotions and
       administrative actions shall be recorded.
   15. Certificate generation must follow the configured project completion and approval rules.
   16. Users must not be able to access private information belonging to unauthorized users or
       projects.

 3.5 Role and Permission Management Rules
 The platform shall implement Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to manage user permissions.
    • Each user account shall have a role assigned by the system or the Super Admin.
    • The backend shall validate user permissions before executing protected operations.
    • The frontend shall display functionality according to the user's permissions.
    • Hiding a button or page in the frontend shall not replace backend authorization.
    • Project-level permissions shall be checked in addition to general role permissions.
    • Unauthorized requests shall be rejected.
    • Role changes, including promotions to and demotions from the Sub Admin role, shall be
       recorded in the system logs.
    • Deactivated accounts shall not be allowed to access protected functionality.
    • AI services shall operate within the access permissions of the requesting user.
